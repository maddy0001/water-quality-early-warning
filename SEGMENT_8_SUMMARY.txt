🚨 4-LEVEL ALERT SYSTEM:
================================================================================

LEVEL 0 - NORMAL (Green ✓)
  Message: "Water quality is normal"
  Action:  Continue routine monitoring
  Triggers:
    • Classification: Potable prediction
    • Forecasting: pH in safe range (6.5-8.5)
    • Anomaly: 0-1 detectors flagged

LEVEL 1 - LOW (Yellow ⚠️)
  Message: "Minor water quality deviation detected"
  Action:  Increase monitoring frequency
  Triggers:
    • Classification: 50-70% non-potable confidence
    • Forecasting: pH deviation ±0.05-0.10
    • Anomaly: 2 detectors flagged

LEVEL 2 - MEDIUM (Orange ⚠️⚠️)
  Message: "Significant concern - investigation recommended"
  Action:  Investigate immediately, notify operators
  Triggers:
    • Classification: 70-90% non-potable confidence
    • Forecasting: pH deviation ±0.10-0.15
    • Anomaly: 3 detectors flagged

LEVEL 3 - CRITICAL (Red 🚨)
  Message: "CRITICAL: Immediate action - water may be contaminated"
  Action:  Emergency protocol, stop distribution
  Triggers:
    • Classification: 90%+ non-potable confidence
    • Forecasting: pH deviation >±0.15
    • Anomaly: All 4 detectors flagged

🧮 ALERT AGGREGATION (WEIGHTED VOTING):
================================================================================

Multiple alert sources → Single unified alert

Weights:
  • Classification: 40%
  • Forecasting:    30%
  • Anomaly:        30%

Example:
  Classification: MEDIUM (level=2, weight=0.4) → 0.8
  Forecasting:    LOW    (level=1, weight=0.3) → 0.3
  Anomaly:        MEDIUM (level=2, weight=0.3) → 0.6
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Weighted sum: 1.7
  Average:      1.7 / 1.0 = 1.7
  Final level:  MEDIUM (rounded from 1.7)

Special rule: If ANY source is CRITICAL → elevate to at least MEDIUM

**Section 6: Early Warning System**

"A four-level alert system was implemented to provide real-time water quality 
monitoring and contamination warnings. Alert levels (NORMAL, LOW, MEDIUM, 
CRITICAL) are determined through weighted aggregation of predictions from 
classification, forecasting, and anomaly detection subsystems.

Alert Aggregation Strategy:
Multiple detection sources provide complementary information. A weighted 
voting mechanism (Classification: 40%, Forecasting: 30%, Anomaly: 30%) 
combines alerts into a unified risk assessment. This multi-model fusion 
reduces false positives while maintaining high sensitivity.

Thresholds:
- Classification: 90%+ confidence → CRITICAL
- Forecasting: pH deviation >0.15 units → CRITICAL  
- Anomaly: 4/4 detectors flagged → CRITICAL

Alert Processing:
All alerts are logged with timestamps, location, confidence, and contributing 
sources. CRITICAL and MEDIUM alerts trigger immediate notifications to 
operators. A dashboard provides real-time risk scoring (0-100) based on 
recent alert patterns.
