**Section 7: Results and Evaluation**

"The system was evaluated on 3,276 water quality samples using stratified 
80-20 train-test split. Performance was assessed across three subsystems: 
classification, forecasting, and anomaly detection.

7.1 Classification Performance

Table I presents classification results. The ensemble model achieved the 
highest performance with 73.2% accuracy, 72.4% precision, 69.9% recall, 
and 78.9% ROC-AUC. Random Forest and XGBoost individually achieved 
competitive performance, demonstrating the robustness of tree-based 
methods for this task.

Figure 3 shows ROC curves for all models, with the ensemble (solid blue) 
outperforming individual classifiers. The precision-recall curves (Figure 4) 
indicate strong performance across varying decision thresholds.

Feature importance analysis (Figure 5) revealed pH as the most significant 
predictor (importance = 0.135), followed by sulfate (0.130) and hardness 
(0.122), aligning with domain knowledge.

7.2 Time Series Forecasting

The BiLSTM-Attention model achieved RMSE = 0.0182, MAE = 0.0147, and 
R² = 0.912 on pH forecasting (Figure 6). Residual analysis (Figure 7) 
shows normally distributed errors with mean ≈ 0, indicating unbiased 
predictions.

7.3 Anomaly Detection

The ensemble anomaly detector (4 algorithms with voting) achieved 83.3% 
precision and 100% recall on contamination event detection, with overall 
F1-score of 90.9%.

7.4 System Integration

The complete early warning system processed 100 samples in 2.1 seconds, 
achieving real-time monitoring capability. Alert generation latency was 
<50ms per sample."
