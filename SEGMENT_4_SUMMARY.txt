🔍 HOW EACH MODEL WORKS (For Paper):
================================================================================

RANDOM FOREST:
  - 200 decision trees trained on random subsets
  - Each tree votes → majority wins
  - Best for: interpretability, feature importance
  - Weakness: may overfit without balancing

XGBOOST:
  - Builds trees sequentially (each fixes previous errors)
  - Gradient descent optimization
  - Best for: accuracy, speed
  - Weakness: more hyperparameters to tune

SVM (RBF Kernel):
  - Finds optimal hyperplane in high-dimensional space
  - RBF kernel handles non-linear boundaries
  - Best for: small-medium datasets
  - Weakness: slow on large datasets

ENSEMBLE (Soft Voting):
  - Combines probability outputs of all models
  - Weighted: RF(0.35) + XGB(0.40) + SVM(0.25)
  - Best for: robustness, error compensation
  - Reduces individual model weaknesses
